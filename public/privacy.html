Privacy notice

Anareen Lifecare Hospital uses this website to give out OP tokens and show your place in the queue. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep about you and why.

What we store

  • Patient's name: when you book online, or if you give it at the reception desk.
  • Mobile number: so you can find your tokens on "My tokens".
  • Token details: which doctor, the date, your token number, whether you booked online or at the desk, and whether you were seen, cancelled or did not come.
  • The time you agreed to this notice.

We do not ask for your address, age, medical history or any payment details. This website does not keep medical records.

Why we store it

  • To give you a token and keep the queue in order.
  • To let you check your turn, and cancel your token, using your mobile number.
  • So reception and your doctor know who is next.
  • To count how many patients we see (these counts don't include names or numbers).

We do not sell your details or share them for advertising, and we don't send marketing messages.

How long we keep it

Your name and mobile number are deleted automatically 30 days after your visit. After that we keep only counts that can't be linked back to you (for example, "Dr. Arun saw 40 patients on 3 March").

For safety, the hospital keeps backup copies of the system from the last 14 days, so a deleted name can stay in a backup for up to 14 more days before it is gone for good.

Who can see it

  • Reception and admin staff: your full name and mobile number, to manage tokens.
  • Your doctor: your name and token number on their screen.
  • Anyone who enters your mobile number on "My tokens": your token, your doctor and a shortened name (like "Ra*** K."). Your mobile number is never shown. To cancel, they would also need your 4-digit booking code, so keep it to yourself.
  • The waiting-hall screen: only token numbers and doctors' names, never patients' names.

On your phone

If you choose, this website remembers your mobile number and your language on your own phone, so you don't have to type them again. You can remove them with "Change number", or by clearing your browser's site data. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • You can get a token at the reception desk without giving a name or mobile number.
  • You can ask reception to correct or delete your details at any time.
  • Questions? Call us on +91 98765 43210 or ask at the reception desk.
